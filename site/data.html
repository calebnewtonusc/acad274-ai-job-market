Three tables, nine charts

Every number below is computed from our three cleaned CSVs by tools/build.py: Ellen's 831-occupation dataset, Anthropic's observed AI use by job, and the NY Fed's recent-grad outcomes by major. Tables 1 and 2 use the same math as our database views, so they match what exposure.php and fields.php return.

Table 1

By AI exposure level: does the rating line up with growth, pay and real use?

Occupations rated High and Very high pay more on average than Low and Moderate ones, and no level is projected to shrink on average. Observed use stays at 0.060 or below from Low through High, then jumps to 0.241 at Very high.

Interactive ideas

Brainstorm only, nothing committed yet. Each one reads a feed we already have.

Pick your field or major

A dropdown or search box that highlights one field across all nine charts and shows its row from Tables 2 and 3. Reviewers asked for this at M1. Reads fields.php and jobs.php?field=.

Drill down from field to job

Click a bubble in 2c to load that field's occupations into a scatter of observed use against projected change. Reads jobs.php?field=.

Exposure threshold slider

A slider for what counts as "exposed" (Very high only, High and up, Moderate and up) that redraws 2a and the headline share live.

Weighting toggle

Switch between "every occupation counts once" and "weight by 2025 employment". The averages in Tables 1 and 2 change, which shows how much a few large jobs drive the story.

Education and experience filters

Checkboxes for required education and work experience that filter every chart. Tests whether entry jobs are the exposed ones. Reads experience.php.

Prediction vs reality narrative

A scroll story that steps through Low to Very high, revealing observed use one level at a time, ending on the field the reader picked.

JSON feeds

php pages that output our data as JSON

Built by Caleb. Each runs one SELECT on a view in ellenjun_aijobs and returns a JSON array. They run on iyaserver only, since they need the database login in a gitignored config.php.

  • api/fields.php: every field with jobs, employment, average change, average wage and high-exposure share (view field_summary, Table 2).
  • api/exposure.php: one row per AI exposure level, Low to Very high (view exposure_summary, Table 1).
  • api/experience.php: one row per work experience level (view experience_summary).
  • api/jobs.php: every occupation, or one field with ?field=Tech, or one job with ?code=15-1252.
