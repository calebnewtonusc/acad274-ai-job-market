## Questions

1. What's your field or major, and what do you do?
2. What did you (or students in your field) expect AI to do to jobs in it?
3. What have you actually seen happen: hiring, day to day work, pay?
4. Which tasks in your work does AI already do, and which hasn't it touched?
5. When hiring juniors now, what do you look for that you didn't 2 years ago? (pros only)
6. If a student picked your job on our site, what's the one number that would change their decision?
7. We have a government rating that says some jobs are "very high" AI exposure. Does that match what you see?
8. What would make you trust a chart like ours, or ignore it?
