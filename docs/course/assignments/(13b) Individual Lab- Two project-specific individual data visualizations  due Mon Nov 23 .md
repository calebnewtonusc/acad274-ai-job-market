(13b) Individual Lab: Two project-specific individual data visualizations  due Mon Nov 23 18:00

Two individual data visualizations
Working from real project data (from your database/team collected or inputted data), create two data visualizations.
These should be actual rendered visualizations (not sample ones), working off your actual data, but they do NOT need to be live.
So for instance, you could paste the data from query (like in DataGrip) into a spreadsheet, and render charts from the real data. You could also hand-code/enter data into a javascript chart script, and have it render a line graphic of diferent results-- but again the underlying data does not have to be rendered from a live database connection (yet, that is in Milestone3).
The more diverse your data visualization lab examples, the better. I.e. hopefully not just taking data and generating it through a bar and pie chart.
Lab requirements -- web page that contains:

Underlying data in a table or other grid format in the page.
Two examples of data visualizations of the core conclusions or output of the data.
Your visualizations can be rendered from chart libraries, apps (like excel pivot charts), etc. Include a screenshot of your custom visualizations if they are not rendered in the page.

The underlying idea of this lab is that every member of a team is working up ideas for visualizations, from which the team will pick and refine for the data viz requirement of group project Milestone 2. That is also why this lab is due before M2.
This lab (and M2 for that matter) does not require any live renderings, but your final group project will. So any time investments individual students put into connecting live data with visualizations (javascript charts, rendering engines, etc.) will benefit the eventual project... but are NOT required for this lab.
