Data mini-site
For this assignment you will create a multi-page, mini-website on the data analysis from your past 2 assignments. You can repurpose the content (do not have to write brand new analysis or get new datasets), but you should address any suggestions to expand information or analysis from previous assignment feedbacks.
For this assignment you can use/reuse the content/elements from your datagrid/table and interactive assignments.
What you will be doing is create a small/mini website on your topic. Technically a "website" is any series of pages (usually three or four minimum) that have interconnecting links, typically through a navigation. Your assignment will consist of three to four web pages, all interconnected with hyperlinks AND a navigation.
Assignment elements/requirements:

Create a NEW folder/directory (on your computer and eventually on the server) names to the topic, such as SportsStats.
Create three to four webpages on the topic. Suggested breakdown:

Overview Homepage: Introduction to the topic, some background, and high-level takeaways/conclusions. You could certain state your core data assertions here (as motivation to explore your analysis). You could include excepts of the data table here (but probably not the full dataset), but not required for this page.
Data and analysis: One or two pages featuring your core dataset (in table format) as well as analysis (with excerpts/subsets of the data). You could choose to have the full dataset on one page, with some detailed explanation (and perhaps the glossary) and the analysis on another page (with data excerpts), or have two different analysis on different pages, or all on one page.
Resources: Gather all of our resources on the topic into one page. You could for instance break that page down into Tutorials, General Information or Glossary, etc.
Navigation: All pages should have a shared navigation that the user can use to move brtween the pages.
Design: All pages should have generally the same design -- color scheme, tone, and to some extent layout. Although some pages might require specialized version of the layout, they should all have the same general layout area (page location). When you click back and forth between the pages, it should not be jarring and the pages should all look like they are part of the same site.


Suggestions to start:

Create a new folder on your computer AND on the server (file manager > New Folder).
Copy any past assignment pages AND graphics to the new folder. Do not edit the older pages (in their old location) or delete the images in their existing folder.
When done with the assignment, make sure you leave time to test the files once you upload them to the server. Some common issues that may come in troubleshooting include missing/broken images and broken/not working hyperlinks.
