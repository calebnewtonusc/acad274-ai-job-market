Information from Data
During class, you were assigned to a group that researched and surveyed data and information from within a specific domain such as sports, business or medicine.
For this individual lab you will submit text and image (screenshots) isolating in a sub-area of specific sub-domain the following:

Raw data on which it is based
Examples of information published based on that data
Insights or knowledge that can be extracted from the information
Potential "action items" that might come out of above
Suggestions for improved, interactive outputs (data simulations, interactive data viz, etc.)

You CAN include in your submission insights and conclusions from your overall group exercise, but you will be drilling down into a specific/narrow area (versus what your group did).
So for instance if your group analyzed overall player statistics and peformance from all players in the NHL Los Angeles Kings team, you might drill down to performance of defensemen (points, plus/minus, games player, penalty minutes).
You will submit this lab through Brightspace. You can link a doc or paste the text/content directly into the Brightspace submission.
