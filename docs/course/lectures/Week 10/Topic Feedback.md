
Group Topic Feedback/Recommendations

Below are the topic/focus recommendations for each group including consensus green, yellow, red recommendation from the reviwers, supporting arguments, and suggested challenges or isues for each topic.

In some cases there is a clear top recommendation, in others more of a muddle, but in all cases tried to identify challenges/concerns with topics (originality, data availability, clear focus, etc.), and to highlight where was a concensus for original/useful outcomes.

Your group CAN choose to pick one of the topics that was not recommended, but then there will an increased burden on the group to make sure you address all identified challenges with and comments about the topic.

A major milestone for the project is due in just under 2 weeks, so there will not be much time to pivot if you start building out one topic then change your mind.
