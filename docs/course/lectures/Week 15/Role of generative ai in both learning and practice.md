
Use of Generative AI in learning and practice

Developers in the industry are using generative ai as part of their production/workflow, and so in keeping with the Academy ethos of being a professional school that trains students for (various) industries, we need to embrace it.

That of course creates some tensions in the (at times competing) need to have substantive student assessments of their knowledge and skills. Industry professionals do not use generative ai in the beginning, but to increase their productivity and build on top of their existing technical knowledge and skills.

So part of the tension and struggle the Academy (and all university units) are going through is finding that line.

For ACAD-274, the core focus is not explicitly tech learning (like in Dev1, Dev2, Python and Intro to MI, etc) so the balance is even fuzzier.

The call for full ai use transparency has been worked pretty well in ACAD-274, and highlighted very good uses by students in for both design and dev.

The idea is that in the first part of the semester students explicitly implement code samples and approaches provided for the class, and only use ai for debug assistance. Students using ai to completely compile the code for a webpage too early does not give the hands-on experience necessary for the literacy and comprehension that is a core goal of the class. But by the middle of the semester, and especially in the group projects, leaning heavier on ai for a lot of the code is more understandable after students have worked a bit with the various technologies (html, css, jQuery, SQL) and are now focused more on end-results and achieving goals for their data web projects.

What was most difficult for students in trying to work with new code / samples early in the class, which might have driven them to ai? Being honest, was it that forcing through getting their own code to work was too time-consuming? Too difficult? Were not comfortable reaching out for the degree of support they might have needed?

Once students achieved at least a beginner level of facility in most related techs, in what areas did they find generative ai most useful? I.e. if you could build a basic web app page, were there "spot" areas you found it useful to help/improve? I.e. you were looking for design ideas/improvements for an existing page (that you wrote), adding animation to a page, tweaking, improving or fixing a SQL SELECT statement you wrote/started, etc?

One metaphor I really like about AI is thinking of it like an employee. Would you let an employee dictate everything about a project, or bring them in for specific tasks. You also do not want to be too dependent on generative AI. If you have to go back to an ai agent to change a project, that's a problem.
