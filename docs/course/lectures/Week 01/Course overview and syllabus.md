Use of generative AI: 

And a brief note on the use of generative ai in this class, i.e. using chatGPT (et al) to write code. The bottomline is is disclose, disclose, disclose. If you use generative ai in your submissions, which could range from design (html and css) to scripting (javascript, php, sql) you must disclose that in your Brightspace submission. So in other words instead of just submitting the URL of your assignment page, you should then have a couple of sentences explaining that you used ai to help make your design look better, create an animation effect, write the php to flow your database query into the page, get your SQL SELECT statement to the database working, etc. 

In some cases you will have gone further than my instructions/parameters for ai use, but you will not get in trouble (lose points, get zero credit, or if misrepresentation potentially get reported to usc). If you are disclosing, at worst I will ask you not to use in that context again for a submission. 

The good news is that when we get to the group projects, most uses of ai are fine as they will potentially improve your project and parallel how the industry currently operates (increasing the productivity of devs and designers). 

So why restrictions on AI use at all? Well two main reasons: 1) the industry does not hire dev and designers who do not know their core domain, i.e. you have to know your stuff and THEN use ai, and 2) the hands-on experiences and learning will greatly increased your literacy and understanding, even if in some areas you do not feel you learned a particular tech skill or capability sufficient to be considered to have a competency.

Side note: Some lab and regular assignments will have a "Restricted AI" label. For these if you use ai-generated code/assistance you must submit TWO urls -- your original (pre-ai) code as well as the final work. These assignments are intended to make sure you work with and get some familiarity with the associated code, and so while you can use AI to refine and improve the final product you must also show you did some original work first. If you use AI on a Restricted AI assignment and do not submit a pre-AI version you will receive zero credit.
