But instead of going deeper into data theory intro, students groups are going to do a breakout research/discussion on a specific topic, then present the results to the class. NOTE: One member of your team will need to log into the course Zoom room to screenshare.

Group research exercises:

Each group will do a quick research spring on a topic, and be prepared to give a short (2-3 minutes) summary of their results including examples.

Each groups should create and share a google doc, start the doc with their topic and a list of group members, then compile their research and results into the doc including images/screenshots. The doc must also be shared with the instructor (dent@usc.edu).

To present their findings, one rep of the group will walk come up to the front and walk the class through their group's results and conclusions. The group presenter will screenshare the doc / present through zoom.
